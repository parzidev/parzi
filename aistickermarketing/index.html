How it works

From selfie to sticker pack in four steps.

No complicated prompt writing or manual cutouts. StickerAI guides you from a photo to a custom pack.

1. Choose a selfie

Pick a clear, front-facing photo. The app checks the image and prepares it for generation.

2. Pick your reactions

Use a ready-made quick set or select your own 9 expressions from the full collection of 50.

3. Generate your pack

AI creates a consistent 3×3 chibi sheet using your selfie and the 9 expressions you selected.

4. Save and share

Keep the stickers you love, export transparent images, and use them across your favorite messaging apps.
