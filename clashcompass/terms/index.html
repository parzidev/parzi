Free access and Premium

Regular catalogs and offline tools are available without Premium. Free access currently includes one saved player profile, up to 10 visible catalog favorites/personal armies, and one distinct esports layout per UTC calendar week. Reopening the same unlocked layout does not consume another allowance. Premium provides unlimited esports layouts, multiple saved player profiles, unlimited local saves and cloud backup of catalog favorite IDs, subject to technical limits disclosed in the app.

Prices and renewal

Weekly and monthly Premium plans are auto-renewable subscriptions; lifetime Premium is a one-time, non-renewing purchase. The App Store shows the actual price, currency, billing period and any offer before purchase. Apple charges your Apple Account when you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal charges may occur within 24 hours before that end. Manage or cancel a plan in Apple subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore, refunds and cloud identity

Use Tools → Purchases & membership → Restore purchases with the Apple Account that made an active purchase. After purchase verification, an eligible purchase can securely reconnect to the original anonymous app account; use Restore cloud backup on the Premium screen to retrieve saved catalog IDs. This does not migrate your village, drafts, notes or saved player profiles. Pending or deleted accounts and Family Sharing purchases cannot recover someone else’s private app account. If cloud access cannot be verified, contact support. Apple reviews refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com. Canceling a subscription and requesting a refund are separate actions.
