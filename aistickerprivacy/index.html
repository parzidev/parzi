Privacy for StickerAI - Chibi Maker.

This Privacy Policy explains how Parzi handles information related to StickerAI - Chibi Maker and the official StickerAI pages.

Last updated: June 14, 2026 Publisher: Parzi Contact: support@parzi.dev

StickerAI - Chibi Maker is an iOS app published by Parzi. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact support@parzi.dev.

1. Information You Provide

StickerAI may process information you choose to provide while using the app, such as prompts, images, sticker ideas, feedback, or support messages. Do not upload content that you do not have permission to use.

2. App Content and AI Processing

When you ask StickerAI to create chibi-style stickers, the app may need to process the content you provide so the requested sticker can be generated or displayed. Processing may occur on your device or through service providers that help operate app features.

3. Support Communications

If you contact Parzi for support, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message. This information is used to respond to your request and improve support quality.

4. App Store and Device Information

Apple may provide Parzi with app analytics, purchase reporting, crash information, or similar operational data according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. Purchases, refunds, and subscriptions are managed by Apple.

5. How Information Is Used

  • To provide, maintain, and improve StickerAI - Chibi Maker.
  • To generate or display requested chibi sticker content.
  • To respond to support requests sent to support@parzi.dev.
  • To protect the app, users, and Parzi from misuse, abuse, or technical issues.
  • To comply with applicable legal, platform, and safety obligations.

6. Sharing

Parzi does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate StickerAI, with Apple for App Store-related functionality, or when required to comply with law, enforce terms, or protect rights and safety.

7. Data Retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required for legal, security, or operational reasons.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what content you provide to StickerAI. You may also manage Apple App Store subscriptions, purchases, and privacy settings through your Apple account and iOS device settings.

9. Children

StickerAI - Chibi Maker is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use online services without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support@parzi.dev.

10. International Use

StickerAI may be available in multiple regions. Information may be processed in countries other than where you live, subject to applicable legal protections.

11. Changes to This Policy

Parzi may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted at https://parzi.dev/aistickerprivacy/.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Parzi at support@parzi.dev.
