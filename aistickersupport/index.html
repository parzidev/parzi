Getting started

Create your first custom pack.

StickerAI uses a guided flow, so you do not need to write prompts or edit images manually.

1

Choose a clear selfie

Use a front-facing photo with one visible face, good lighting, and as little obstruction as possible.

2

Select exactly 9 reactions

Pick a quick set or choose your own 9 from 50 expression options, including happy, funny, love, sad, angry, and everyday reactions.

3

Generate, save, and share

StickerAI creates a personalized 3×3 pack. Save your favorites, export transparent stickers, or share them in chats.

FAQ

Common support questions.

Why do I need to choose 9 expressions?

Each generation creates a 3×3 pack with 9 stickers. Your selected expressions determine the reaction shown in each position.

Do I need to write an AI prompt?

No. Choose a selfie and your expressions; StickerAI prepares the generation instructions automatically.

How can I share my stickers?

Save transparent images, share through the iOS share sheet, prepare stickers for WhatsApp or iMessage, or access saved stickers from the StickerAI keyboard.

Where are my saved stickers?

Open the My Stickers area in the app to view saved packs, mark favorites, preview individual stickers, and choose an export option.

Where are purchases handled?

Subscriptions and credit packs are purchased through Apple. Billing, refunds, and subscription management follow Apple and App Store policies.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open StickerAI Settings, go to the subscription area, and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I contact support?

Email support@parzi.dev with your device model, iOS version, app version, a screenshot if helpful, and a short description of what happened.

Where can I read legal information?

The official Privacy Policy is at parzi.dev/aistickerprivacy, and the Terms of Use are at parzi.dev/aistickerterms.

Troubleshooting

Before you email.

  • Use a clear photo with one unobstructed face and sufficient lighting.
  • Confirm that your iPhone has a stable internet connection during AI generation.
  • Make sure exactly 9 different expressions are selected before generating.
  • Install the latest available version of StickerAI from the App Store.
  • Restart the app and try again if a generation or purchase status does not refresh.
  • Check your available credits or subscription status in the app before starting another generation.
  • For billing or refund questions, use Apple App Store purchase support.
