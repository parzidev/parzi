FAQ

Common support questions.

How do I contact support?

Email support@parzi.dev with your device model, iOS version, app version if available, and a short description of the issue.

Who publishes the app?

StickerAI - Chibi Maker is published by Parzi.

Where are purchases handled?

App downloads, purchase records, refunds, and subscription management are handled through Apple and the App Store.

Where can I read legal information?

The official Privacy Policy is at parzi.dev/aistickerprivacy, and the Terms of Use are at parzi.dev/aistickerterms.

Troubleshooting

Before you email.

  • Confirm that your iPhone is connected to the internet.
  • Install the latest available version of StickerAI - Chibi Maker from the App Store.
  • Restart the app if a screen does not update as expected.
  • For billing or refund questions, use Apple App Store purchase support.
