Data Deletion

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Use Settings → Delete app data and confirm. This removes favorites, playlists, recent songs, reading preferences and the anonymous Cloudflare account. Connectivity is required to delete the server record. The app reports success only after the deletion request succeeds.

Purchases

Deletion does not cancel or erase your Apple purchase. Restore it using the same Apple Account. Apple and RevenueCat transaction records follow those providers' retention rules and applicable legal requirements.

If you cannot access the app

Email support@parzi.dev with the subject “Akorca data deletion”. Include your support identifier from Settings if available. Do not send an access token, password or card information. We may request the minimum information needed to locate and verify the anonymous record.

Daily song access records are removed after 7 days by a scheduled cleanup. Legally required records and provider backups may remain under the relevant retention rules. See the Privacy Policy.
