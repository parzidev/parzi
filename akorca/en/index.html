Akorca

Last updated: 23 October 2026

An iPhone and iPad app for Turkish song lyrics, guitar chords, transposition and playlists.

Free use includes 10 different songs per calendar day in the Europe/Istanbul time zone. Reopening the same song on that day does not use another view. Lifetime Premium is a single, non-renewing purchase that unlocks unlimited access and offline use of the included catalog.

Availability is shown on the App Store.
