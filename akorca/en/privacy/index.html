Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Akorca is developed by Ali Dinç (Parzi). Contact support@parzi.dev for privacy requests.

Data on your device

Favorites, playlists, recently opened songs and reading preferences stay on your device. No name or email sign-up form is required. A random app identifier and access token are stored in Apple Keychain for secure access and daily usage limits.

Server data

Cloudflare Workers and D1 process the random identifier, a hash of the access token and the IDs of songs opened on each day. This enforces the free daily limit and prevents charging another view for the same song. Song-opening records are retained for 7 days. Short-lived abuse prevention records are removed by a daily cleanup. Cloudflare processes IP addresses as part of the network connection; raw IP addresses are not stored in the app database.

Purchases

Apple processes payments; we do not receive your card or banking details. RevenueCat processes the random app identifier, product and transaction identifiers, purchase/refund status, store/country information and device, OS and app version information needed by its SDK to verify and restore purchases. These records can be associated with the same anonymous app identifier.

See the privacy policies of RevenueCat, Cloudflare and Apple. These providers may process data outside your country, including outside Türkiye.

Diagnostics and support

Apple MetricKit reports are stored locally and are sent to the developer only when you choose to share them. Support messages and attachments are used to address your request. Do not send passwords, card details or access tokens.

Tracking and permissions

The app does not use advertising tracking, an advertising network or cross-site tracking. It does not request contacts, location, microphone or camera access. These documentation pages use no cookies or visitor analytics.

Purpose, retention and your rights

Necessary data is processed to provide the service, fulfill purchases, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. The anonymous server record remains until you use the deletion control. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records under their own policies and legal duties; a separate request to those providers may be needed.

Use Settings → Delete app data to delete local data and your anonymous server record. An internet connection is required. See Data deletion. Contact support for access, correction, deletion and other applicable rights. We request only information needed to verify your request. Deletion does not erase Apple's legal purchase record.

Children and updates

Akorca is not specifically directed at children. Content suitability is stated by its App Store age rating. Material changes to these practices will be reflected on this page.
