Terms of Use

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The Apple Standard End User License Agreement (EULA) governs use of Akorca. This page explains usage and purchases and does not replace the EULA or mandatory consumer rights.

Free use

You can open 10 different songs per calendar day in the Europe/Istanbul time zone. The allowance resets at midnight. Reopening a song on the same day does not consume another view. Free song access requires an internet connection. Search, favorites, playlists and the chord library are available without a purchase.

Lifetime Premium

Akorca Premium is one non-consumable, non-expiring, non-renewing purchase. It is not a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription. It removes the song-opening limit and enables offline use of the song content bundled with the app. Initial purchase and restore require an internet connection. New content and service updates may require connectivity.

Pricing and payment

The actual price and currency appear in Apple's purchase confirmation before payment. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. Regional prices and taxes can differ. The app offers a single premium product.

Restore and refunds

Use the same Apple Account and Settings → Premium → Restore purchases. Restoring does not transfer device-local favorites or playlists. Apple handles refund requests. Refunded or revoked transactions may lose premium access.

Content rights

Lyrics, compositions, artist names and other third-party content belong to their respective owners. Premium does not transfer ownership or redistribution rights. Do not unlawfully copy or distribute content. Rights holders can use the Copyright notice process. Content may be corrected or removed.

Availability and support

Network failures, maintenance and provider changes can affect online features. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected. Contact support@parzi.dev for assistance.
