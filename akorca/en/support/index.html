Support

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Email support@parzi.dev with your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description. Remove private information from screenshots. Never send an Apple password, card number or access token.

Daily song allowance

Free access includes 10 different songs each day and resets at midnight in Istanbul. Reopening the same song that day is free. Settings shows the remaining allowance. Free song access requires connectivity.

Premium and restore

Premium is a one-time purchase and does not renew. Use the same Apple Account and Settings → Premium → Restore purchases if your purchase is missing. The initial verification requires connectivity. Bundled song content then works offline.

Refunds and privacy

Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Read the Privacy Policy and Data deletion information. Include the song and artist when reporting incorrect chords or content rights concerns.
